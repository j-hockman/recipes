Quick and Easy BLT Pizza

Ingredients for BLT Pizza:

  • Pizza Crust:

    This recipe does just fine with a convenient prebaked pizza crust. However, if you want to make one yourself, a homemade dough will work as well!

  • Miracle Whip:

    The sweetness and spice in Miracle Whip taste great in this pizza sauce, but you can use regular mayo if you prefer.

  • Seasonings:

    We season Miracle Whip with basil, garlic and onion to make the base pizza sauce.

  • Bacon:

    Use your favorite style and cut of bacon. And sure, turkey bacon counts!

  • Cheese:

    We use equal amounts of shredded cheddar and mozzarella cheeses. Cheese that you grate yourself always tastes and melts better than pre-shredded cheese, but pre-shredded is perfectly fine (and easier) to use.

  • Lettuce and Tomato:

    It wouldn’t be a BLT without these. Use shredded crisp romaine or iceberg lettuce to top the pizza, or go a different route with pretty leafy greens like kale and baby arugula. For tomatoes, use ripe on-the-vine tomatoes, heirloom tomatoes if they’re in season, or halved cherry or grape tomatoes.

Directions:

  1. Make the sauce

    2. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Place the crust in an ungreased 12-inch pizza pan. In a small bowl, stir together the Miracle Whip, basil, garlic powder and onion powder. Spread the sauce over the crust.

  2. Bake the pizza

    3. Set aside 1/4 cup bacon. Sprinkle the cheeses and remaining bacon on top of the pizza. Bake for 8 to 12 minutes or until the cheese gets melty and bubbly.

  3. Top with lettuce & tomatoes

    4. Top the pizza with lettuce, tomatoes and reserved bacon. Cut pizza into wedges, and serve.
