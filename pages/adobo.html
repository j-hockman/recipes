Spicy Chicken Adobo

Ingredients for Spicy Chicken Adobo:

  • 16oz chicken breast

    Be sure to chop the chicken breast into half inch strips.

  • Garlic Cloves

    You will need 2 raw garlic cloves diced.

  • Vinegar and Soy Sauce

    You will need a half cup of vinegar and a full cup of soy sauce.

  • Red Chili Pepper

    Get yourself one large dried red chili pepper and finely dice it.

  • White Rice

    You will need one full cup of white rice.

Directions:

  1. Cook the rice

    2. Put your rice into a 2qt pot and fill it with water until it is level with your second knuckle, then boil until the rice is done.

  2. Make the sauce

    3. Mix the vinegar, soy sauce, diced garlic, and diced red chili pepper together into a medium sized wok and bring to a simmer.

  3. Cook the chicken

    4. After the sauce has been brough to a simmer add the strips of chicken breast to the sauce and sautee on medium heat until the chicken is white all the way through but still tender.

  4. Make the bowl

    5. Put your cooked rice into a dinner bowl then put the sauteed chicken on top (optional: grab a spoon and drizzle the sauce from the pan over the rice and chicken for a stronger flavor).
