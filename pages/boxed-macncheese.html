Chicken Mac and Cheese

Ingredients for Chicken Mac and Cheese:

  • Kraft boxed shells and cheese

    Grab yourself two boxes of Kraft brand shells and cheese.

  • Chicken breast

    You'll need 24oz of shredded chicken breast.

  • Shredded Sharp cheddar cheese

    Grab yourself two cups of your favorite brand of shredded sharp cheddar cheese.

  • Bread crumbs

    Youll need about a half a cup of bread crumbs

Directions:

  1. Cook the chicken

    2. In a 4qt pot, add your shredded chicken breast and fill with water one inch above the level of chicken, then boil on medium/high until cooked thoroughly

  2. Cook the Macaroni

    3. Follow the instructions on the box of Kraft shells and cheese to make it as instructed.

  3. Mix the chicken and mac

    4. Mix the chicken (after draining it) to the shells and cheese in a medium glass baking pan then top it off with the shredded cheddar and spread the breadcrumbs evenly across the top.

  4. Bake it up

    5. Place the glass pan into an oven preheated to 350 and let it bake for 10 minutes.
